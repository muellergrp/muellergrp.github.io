<h3>Bio</h3>

I am a first-year Public Health major at UCLA with a strong interest in infectious disease dynamics and health equity. I am excited to be part of the Müller Lab, where I will be analyzing measles virus data using BEAST analysis to estimate sampling parameters through birth–death skyline simulations. At UCLA, I am also involved in student organizations dedicated to public health advocacy. In the future, I hope to advance health equity through research and interventions focused on infectious disease transmission and control.
